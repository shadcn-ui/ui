The official website for shadcn/ui is [ui.shadcn.com](https://ui.shadcn.com).

## Official resources

- **Website:** [ui.shadcn.com](https://ui.shadcn.com) is the official website and component documentation.
- **Source code:** [github.com/shadcn-ui/ui](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui) is the official source repository for shadcn/ui and the CLI.
- **CLI package:** [npmjs.com/package/shadcn](https://www.npmjs.com/package/shadcn) is the official `shadcn` package published on npm.
- **Project updates:** [@shadcn](https://twitter.com/shadcn) publishes official announcements and project updates.

## Third-party resources

Community websites and registries may use shadcn/ui components or the shadcn CLI, but they are independent unless they are explicitly identified as maintained by shadcn/ui on this page.

Review third-party code before installing it in your project. Browse community-maintained registries in the official [Registry Directory](/docs/directory).
